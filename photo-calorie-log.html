How it works

  1. Snap. Take one or more photos of the meal. A photo added later tops up the estimate; it does not redo it.
  2. Review. Mealog lists each food it found with an estimated portion, calories and macros.
  3. Correct and save. Tap any item to change a number. Your own figure always wins over the AI's.

An estimate, not a measurement

A photo cannot show oil, sauces or exactly how much is on the plate. Mealog says so: totals that rest on unchecked entries are shown with a tilde (for example ~1,577 kcal), and low-confidence items are flagged for review.

Your photos

If you agree to AI estimation (the app asks first, and you can withdraw at any time), the photo is sent to OpenAI in the US through Mealog's own proxy, only to produce the estimate. Your log stays on your iPhone; iCloud sync is optional and off by default. See the privacy policy.
