1. What Mealog is

Mealog estimates the nutritional content of a meal from a photo, a spoken description or typed text, and keeps a log of those estimates for you.

Mealog is not medical, dietary or nutritional advice. The numbers it produces are AI estimates, not measurements. Do not rely on them for any medical decision — including insulin dosing, managing an allergy, a clinical diet or treating any condition. If a decision about your health depends on precise nutrition, weigh and verify your food and speak to a doctor or a registered dietitian.

2. Accuracy

Estimates are generated by an AI model from limited information: a photo cannot show what is inside a sauce, and a spoken sentence cannot convey a portion size precisely. Accuracy varies with the dish, the lighting, the angle and how typical the recipe is. Every entry is editable, and correcting an entry is the intended way to make your log reflect what you actually ate.
