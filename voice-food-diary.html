How it works

  1. Speak. Describe the meal. If you mention a time, the entry gets that time.
  2. Check the transcript. Mealog shows what it heard so you can fix a word before the meal is analysed.
  3. Review and save. Adjust any estimated number, then save. Your own figure always wins over the AI's.

An estimate, not a measurement

Spoken portions are approximate, so the numbers are estimates. Totals resting on unchecked entries are shown with a tilde, and low-confidence items are flagged for review.

Your voice

If you agree to AI estimation (the app asks first), the recording is sent to OpenAI in the US only to produce the transcript, then deleted from your iPhone once it has been transcribed. Only the text is kept. See the privacy policy.
